Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Qamra (“we”) is an Egyptian creative and production house. This policy explains what we collect when you contact us through shotonqamra.com, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or our ads, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email.
  • Your business or brand name, and the project details you choose to share with us.
  • Basic, anonymous website usage data, such as pages visited.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, prepare a quote and work on your project.
  • To follow up about your request. We do not send marketing messages unless you agree.

Sharing

We never sell your information. It is handled only by the tools we use to run our work, such as Meta (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp), Google and our form provider, and only for the purposes above.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while we are talking or working together, and for up to two years after that, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your information. Email info@shotonqamra.com or message us on WhatsApp.
