Why Qamra

The camera was born in a dark room.

A thousand years ago, Ibn al-Haytham showed that light passing through a small hole into a dark room paints the world upside down on the wall. That room was called the qamra.

قمرة→Camera

Qamra is a Cairo production house that still works that way: light in, story out.

Founded by Mohamed Tarek, photographer, filmmaker and host of Tarek’s Take.
